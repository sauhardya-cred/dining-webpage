# Dining website — folds 1–3 (desktop)

- **The start:** the site opens on black and `fold 1.mp4` plays from 0:00 to 0:04 by itself as it fades in: the curtains open on the landing view and "THE *Best Kitchens* IN TOWN / ARE EXPECTING *You.*" comes in (`LANDING_IN`). The page rests there; the first scroll takes it on down to the top view of the table (the second stop, the line still on screen), and the next goes on to the dining pass, the line leaving as the camera starts down into the table. Scrolling up from the landing view rewinds the opening to the closed curtains.
- **Landing (load animation):** starts once the first 4 s of `fold 1.mp4` have loaded. Scrolling is locked until it finishes.
- **On scroll** (everything scrubs forwards and backwards with the scrollbar), from the landing view:
  1. Scrolling **up** rewinds the opening back to the closed curtains (0:00).
  2. Scrolling **down** plays the rest of `fold 1.mp4`, ending on the top view of the table.
  1. **The bill book** (after the green path in `FOLD1 camera.png`): one scroll drops the camera from the top view down to the table, shrinking it to the size of a mouse at the start of the path, then, in one continuous shot, it glides along the path a few centimetres above the wood, past the giant spoon and the platter, along the huge knife, and stops by the towering enoki. Nothing on the table moves (the spoon and the wine glasses included): they stay where they are, only changing with the camera's perspective, and are enormous next to it. Without any cut, in that same view, a giant hand comes down and places a slim closed leather bill folder on the table in the left half, with the dining pass (`Dining pass.png`) tucked between its covers so the pass's coin peeks out, then lifts away, and "introducing, dining pass." comes in on the right half (with a soft dark glow so it reads over the painting). Every frame is painted in fold 1's style. Made with Higgsfield: Nano Banana 2.1 painted the mouse-height start of the path, Seedance 2.5 made the drop to the table and then the glide, stop and hand as a single 10 s take from that start (no painted end frame to steer toward, so nothing morphs into a different scene). The coin is gone.
  2. **Fold 3:** the background fades to white, and the hand from `Fold 3/Fold 3 hand.png` slides in from the bottom-left (ease-out, turning 12° into place) holding up the credit card as if offered for payment, and rests where the card sits in `Fold 3/Fold 3 hand ref.png`. The headline and then the body copy slide up and fade in to where they sit in `fold3.jpg`. The hand (`layers/hand.webp`) is painted in an illustrative style with soft, thin warm-brown outlines, after the reference hand placing the bill book (the outlines softened by blending them into the colour around them; earlier versions: `hand_oil_style.webp`, `hand_smooth_style.webp`, `hand_outlined_sharp.webp`) and holds the blue-to-pink gradient card, painted into the same image, as it was when the outlines were softened (`higgsfield outputs/hand_painted/hand_soft_gradient_card.webp`). The later crisp card layers are kept in `higgsfield outputs/hand_painted/`: the striped card, `Card aw.png` (`card_aw_layer.webp`) and `Fold 3/Card gradient.png` (`card_gradient_layer.webp`), each with the hand it was cut out of and the builder `cardgen.js`. The light sweep (`drawCardSweep()`, `CARD_SWEEP`) only runs when a `card` entry is in `LAYERS`. (Higgsfield: Nano Banana 2.1, upscaled, background removed, then fitted onto the old hand's outline so it sits in exactly the same place; the generations and the old hand are in `higgsfield outputs/hand_painted/`).
  3. **Collage (one scroll):** the hand comes in with the card and, as it comes to rest, with no pause, a timed sequence runs: the eight images from `IMAGE SCROLL`, plus the realistic gold coin from `Dining badge 2.png` (`layers/collage/badge.webp`), pop in one at a time, 65 ms apart, each appearing instantly at its final place (no movement, no fade), building the diagonal collage laid out after `Fold 3/fold 3 new ref.png`. Scrolling back up past that point resets it, ready to run again.
  4. **Facade close-up:** the next scroll brings the rectangular facade stamp to the front and zooms it up in deliberate low-frame-rate steps (8 fps, about 1.1 s), straightening it as it grows. Partway through, the stamp picture hard-cuts (no fade) to a window onto `Buidling facade.jpg`, and the zoom ends with that image filling the screen. Scrolling back up zooms it back out into the collage (same steps, in reverse). If the user scrolls on before the collage has finished popping in, the zoom waits for it.
  5. **Through the gate:** the next scroll flies the camera into the facade toward the centre gate: the camera travels one straight line at constant speed (after a brief ease in from rest), about 1.65 s, never stopping. The pillars, canopy and wall are separate layers at different depths, projected with real perspective, so the pillars sweep out fastest, the canopy rises away a little slower and the wall moves least, which reads as 3D space. As the camera approaches, the two doors swing inward in perspective onto a dark doorway, and the camera carries straight on through the doorway into the gallery (fold 4, after `Fold 4.mp4`), which sits just behind the doors close to its final size and settles, with a little parallax between its layers (back wall, framed painting, side walls and cornice, floor), into the framing of `Fold4/Fold4.png`: the frame on the right, holding the painting from `fold4.2 bg.png`, and on the left "YOUR BILL / ARRIVES WITH / GOOD NEWS" with its body under it. About 2.9 s. Fold 4 is a single view.
  6. **Into the painting:** after fold 4, the camera pushes into the framed painting as if through a window, the frame growing past the edges of the screen until the painting fills it (`SEG.dive`, `dive.pos`; about 1.6 s when the magnetic scroll carries it). The map then unfolds over it.
  7. **Fold 5, the map** (after `Fold5/Fold 5.mp4`): the next scroll unfolds the painted city map (`Fold5/Map painted.png`) over the gallery from the bottom-right corner, a tilted flap swinging in behind a fold line that sweeps to the top-left, curling as it flattens out (about 0.55 s). About 1.55 s in all; scrolling back up plays it in reverse. The map is now `Fold5/new map.png` (upscaled with Higgsfield to 4096 × 2313, same layout as before) and the pins `Fold5/map pin.png`; the previous map and pin are in `higgsfield outputs/fold5_new_map/`. The pins carry a fixed film-like grain over them (`grainFill()`, strength `FOLD5.pin.grainFill`).
  8. **Fold 6, the receipts** (after `copy placement/6.png` and `6.2.png`): the next scroll slides a white page up over the map, then the receipt from `Fold6/Receipt.png` pops in rolled up at the top of the screen (no fade, like the collage) and unrolls downward on the left, the roll travelling down and thinning as it pays out paper, and three more pop in and unroll beside it in quick succession (`bills`, `pitch`, `stagger`). Before the last one is out, the view zooms in on the first one's total, the bill turning 20° anticlockwise: the zoom is a camera move over all of them, and the others, nearer the camera, sweep out of the frame to the upper right as it moves, in parallax, the further ones faster (`zoom`, `zoomTo`, `parallax`; framed as in `6.2.png` so the foot of the bill stays in view). The total counts down from ₹4,149.00 to ₹3,319.00 (both TOTAL lines), the digits cut from the receipt's own print. Then the "THANK YOU FOR VISITING / barcode / CRED Dining Pass" block rolls up out of sight within its own patch of the bill, and "THE LAST COURSE / IS ON US." rolls up into it, set from the receipt's own printed letters (cut from the CARDHOLDER, AUTH CODE and THANK YOU lines, turned into ink and scaled up to the size in `6.2.png`), so it looks printed on the bill (`FOLD6.swap`, `makePrint()`). The body comes in on the right after the zoom. About 3.4 s (the total starts counting down half a second after the zoom lands); scrolling back up plays it all in reverse.
  9. **Fold 7, the last** (`Fold 7/fold 7 bg.mp4`, copy after `Fold 7 ref.png`): the next scroll wipes the video up over fold 6 the same way, softly blurred as in the reference, playing on loop; "bon appetit." and "the table is yours." slide up and fade in, centred on the screen and centre-aligned. The video only plays while it is on screen, and starts loading while fold 6 plays. Scrolling back up wipes it back down.

Everything is drawn on one pinned `<canvas>`.

**Everything follows the scroll.** From the landing view every frame of every fold and transition sits at its own spot along the scroll: scroll and the scene moves, stop and it stops, scroll back and it runs backwards, with a light smoothing so a mouse wheel's steps glide (`SMOOTH`). Each part has its own stretch of scroll, in order (`SEG`): the top view of the table → the camera gliding through the dishes to the bill book and the hand placing it, "introducing, dining pass." → fold 3, the hand offering the card, and the image pop-ups → the facade zoom and the way through the gate into the gallery, fold 4, "your bill arrives with good news" → the push into the painting → fold 5, the map → fold 6, the receipt → fold 7 "bon appetit.", with short gaps between the parts to read the text that comes with each.

**Magnetic scroll.** When the scrolling stops (trackpad momentum included), the page eases on to the next fold with a text block in the direction it was scrolling (down: the next one below; up: the next one above): the landing view with the landing line (where the page starts) → the top view of the table → the dining pass → fold 3 with the collage in → fold 4 → the map → the counted-down receipt → bon appetit. (`RESTS`). While the snap carries the page, each part it passes plays at a speed that can be followed (`NATURAL_S`: the landing view down to the top of the table 1.6 s, the camera glide 2 s and the bill book 1.6 s, the facade zoom and the way through the gate about 4.7 s, the map about 2 s, the receipt about 3.3 s), the pauses between parts briskly (`GAP_PACE`), easing in as it sets off and out as it arrives, a little quicker going back up. While you scroll yourself, the scene follows the scroll exactly. Dragging the scrollbar settles the same way when it's let go, and any wheel, touch or key input during a settle stops it at once (`SETTLE_IDLE_MS`).

**Scrolling up replays everything exactly in reverse**, at whatever speed you scroll, since every part is tied to the scroll position. The artwork is not retouched.

## Tweaks (`main.js` unless noted)

- How much scrolling a timeline second takes: `SCROLL_VH_PER_S`; how closely the scene follows the scroll: `SMOOTH`; where the page snaps to: `RESTS`; how fast a snap plays each part: `NATURAL_S`, `GAP_PACE`.

- Total scroll length: set from the timeline (`SEG.end` × `SCROLL_VH_PER_S`).
- The start: when the landing line comes in `LANDING_IN` (seconds into fold 1, during the landing animation); how long the landing animation takes to fade in from black `INTRO_FADE_MS`; where it stops `INTRO_END`.
- Where the landing animation stops: `INTRO_END` (frame index; 24 frames = 1 s).
- Copy position and size: the `.ln--*` rules in `styles.css`; when it shows: `updateCopy()` in `main.js`.
- The collage's stretch: `SEG.collage`; the beat before the first pop: `COLLAGE_START`; gap between pops: `COLLAGE_INTERVAL_MS`.
- Where the hand rests (centre, rotation, scale): `LAYERS.arm.place`.
- Collage layout (position, width, rotation of each image, and pop order, after `Fold 3/Fold 3 ref.png`): `COLLAGE`.
- Fold 3 on desktop: the collage images shy away from the mouse, as if magnetically repelled, each pushed straight away from the cursor, the nearer the more, 10px at most, easing in and back (`REPEL`: `max`, `reach`, `ease`; a mouse only, not on phones or with reduced motion).
- Fold 3 on desktop keeps the copy clear: any collage image whose painted pixels come within 15px (`COLLAGE_CLEAR`) of the heading or the line under it is shrunk about its own centre (to 70% at most, `COLLAGE_MIN_FIT`), then moved up or down away from it if that isn't enough; worked out per screen size (`collageFit()`), so it holds on any screen shape. The copy itself is unchanged.
- Facade close-up: its stretch `SEG.facade`; `FACADE_ZOOM_MS` is its length when the snap plays it; when the picture cuts to the close-up `FACADE_CUT`. It now moves smoothly at the screen's frame rate, like the gate's parallax after it; it used to step at 8 fps.
- Fold 7 (`FOLD7`): timing of the wipe and the copy; its stretch `SEG.f7`; the background's blur and brightness: `.f7bg` in `styles.css`.
- Fold 6 (`FOLD6`): the receipt's centre, top and height `rest`, the roll's radius `roll`, where the zoom ends and how far it turns `zoomTo`, the total's place, values, digit cells and the glyphs it is reprinted from `total`, the timing (`wipe`, `drop`, `unroll`, `zoom`, `count`, `head`, `body`, `ms`); the roll's look is `drawRoll()`; its stretch `SEG.f6`.
- Fold 5 (`FOLD5`): what fills the screen `view` (map px), the pins (`pins`, tip positions in the order they pop), the card size and slant (`card.slant`), the width of the pins' grainy edge (`pin.grain`), and the timing (`unfold`, `cardIn`, `copyAt`, `pinsAt`, `ms`); its stretch `SEG.f5`.
- The gallery (`GALLERY`): its framing `views[0]` (room px); each layer's distance `d` from where the camera stops (smaller = nearer = more parallax, larger = the gallery shows bigger through the doors); the push into the painting `SEG.dive`.
- Through the gate: its stretch `SEG.gate`; when the doors open during the move `GATE_DOORS_OPEN`; how long the camera takes to get up to speed `GATE_EASE_IN`; how close it gets `GATE_END_SCALE`; how far the doors swing `DOOR_OPEN_DEG`; each layer's depth `z` in `F3D` (smaller = nearer = moves faster).
- The order and length of every part, and the reading gaps between them: `SEG` (built with `add(name, gap, length)`); fold 2's glide and hand placement: `F2`.

## Mobile

Under 768px wide the site switches to a phone layout, after `Dining Folds bg/mob/` (`FOLD1.png` … `FOLD7.png`, 390 × 844 screens). The folds, the order, the timing and the scrolling are the same; only the layout changes, and it switches back if the window widens. Positions are in the references' px, scaled to fit the screen (by its width or height, whichever is tighter, so nothing is cut off on short phones) and centred; the backgrounds (frames, gallery, map, video) still fill the whole screen.

The stage is pinned to the screen (`position: fixed`) and is always exactly the visible height (`100dvh`, redrawn as a phone's address bar slides in and out), so there is never a band below it and it never runs past the bottom of the screen; the scroll is measured against the steady small-viewport height (`.vh-probe`), so the folds don't jump when the address bar moves, and the browser's pull-to-refresh bounce is turned off (`overscroll-behavior`).

- Copy: every line placed and sized as in its reference (the headings measured off them: the landing line Adelphe 19.4 with Pinyon 31.2, 20% larger than the reference; "INTRODUCING" 16 over "Dining Pass." 62.5; fold 3 Adelphe 28 with Pinyon 43.5; fold 4 Adelphe 28; the map Roboto Mono 48.8 and 19.3; fold 7 "THE TABLE IS" 22 over "Yours." 35.7), body at 16 on 19.2, broken where the references break it (`br.m`, phones only; `br.d`, desktop only). In the `@media (max-width: 767px)` block in `styles.css`.

- Fold 3: the hand and the collage placed after `mob/FOLD3.png`, the collage below the heading (`MOBILE.arm`, `MOBILE.collage`), the plate 20% larger and turned 5° clockwise, the gold coin drawn over everything (`top`); the first image pops as the card comes to rest.
